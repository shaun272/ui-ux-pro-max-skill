The system

Five zones of control along every belt.

01 / 05
ZONE 01 — LOAD & TRANSFER

Where many conveyor problems begin.

Material drop forces damage the belt and misalign the load. Control impact and containment at the transfer point and the rest of the system inherits a stable belt.

Impact bedsSkirting & sealingDust control
See load-zone components
ZONE 02 — CARRY-SIDE

Stabilise the belt after loading.

Mistracking wanders into spillage, edge damage and structural wear. Carry-side trackers and engineered idlers hold the belt centred at any tonnage.

Belt trackersIdlers & frames
See carry-side components
ZONE 03 — DISCHARGE

Carryback begins at the discharge point.

Material that clings past the head pulley becomes buildup, spillage and housekeeping risk. Primary and secondary cleaners remove it where it starts.

Primary cleanersSecondary cleaners
See discharge components
ZONE 04 — RETURN-SIDE

The return run carries the consequences.

Whatever cleaning misses, the return-side spreads along the gantry. Return trackers and plows protect pulleys and keep the tail drift-free.

Return trackersV-plows
See return-side components
ZONE 05 — MONITORING

Visibility before damage compounds.

Rip detection and belt scales convert the belt from a blind spot into a measured asset — catching the failure before it cascades into the next one.
